Science is how humanity builds reliable knowledge about the world, and keeping a record of what you learn from it is one of the most rewarding uses of a personal wiki. This section is where you collect plain-language summaries of concepts that clicked, discoveries that surprised you, and research that changed how you think. Whether you're following a paper trail in biology, working through cosmology for the first time, or tracking findings in behavioral psychology, this topic turns scattered reading into a coherent, growing body of personal scientific literacy.

## Science Entry Format

Use this template to keep your science entries consistent, well-sourced, and easy to review later:

```md
## [Concept or Discovery]
**Discipline:** Physics / Biology / etc.
**Source:** Paper, book, or article.
**Date Added:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Summary:** Plain-language explanation.
**Why It Matters:** Significance or application.
**Further Reading:** Links.
```

<Note>
  Always cite your source when adding a scientific entry. Science moves fast, and a link back to the original paper, book, or reputable article lets you check whether findings have been updated, replicated, or revised — which is especially important for fields like nutrition, psychology, and climate research.
</Note>
