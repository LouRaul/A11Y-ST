Your personal toolkit is one of the highest-leverage things you can invest in and maintain. The right tool in the right context removes friction, saves time, and lets you focus on the thinking rather than the mechanics. This page is your running record of tools that have earned their place — what they do, why you use them, and what the tradeoffs are, so you can make informed decisions when your needs change.

## Tool Entry Format

Use this template when adding a new tool. Honest notes on pros and cons are more useful than marketing copy — write what you actually experience using it.

```md
## [Tool Name]
**Category:** Productivity / Writing / Dev / etc.
**Website:** https://...
**Cost:** Free / Freemium / Paid
**Use Case:** What you use it for.
**Pros:** ...
**Cons:** ...
```

<Tip>
  Audit your tool stack every quarter. Remove tools you haven't opened in 90 days, consolidate overlapping tools into one, and check whether a free tool you outgrew now has a better paid alternative — or vice versa. A lean, intentional toolkit beats a bloated one every time.
</Tip>
