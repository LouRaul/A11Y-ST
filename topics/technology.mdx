Technology is one of the most expansive and fast-moving domains you can track in a personal knowledge wiki. This section is where you collect notes on software tools you've evaluated, programming concepts you've worked through, AI developments worth remembering, and hardware setups you've refined. Rather than letting browser tabs and scattered bookmarks hold your technical knowledge, this topic gives every insight a permanent, searchable home — one that grows alongside your practice.

## How to Add Technology Entries

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the subtopic">
    Decide which category your entry belongs to — Software Development, AI, Cybersecurity, or Hardware. If it spans more than one, pick the primary angle and cross-link to the others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a brief summary of what you've learned">
    In your own words, explain what the concept or tool is and why it caught your attention. Aim for clarity over completeness — you can always expand later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to related resources or articles">
    Attach the documentation, article, video, or book that taught you this. A note without a source is harder to revisit and verify when details fade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tags for discoverability">
    Tag your entry with relevant keywords — language names, tool categories, or concepts — so you can surface it easily when searching across the wiki.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Technology Note Template

Use this template as a starting point whenever you add a new technology entry:

```md
## [Technology / Concept Name]
**Subtopic:** Software / AI / Security / etc.
**Date Added:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Summary:** What it is and why it matters.
**How I Use It:** Personal application or relevance.
**Resources:** Links to articles, books, or tools.
```

<Tip>
  When you document a tool here, link it to the corresponding entry in your **Resources** section. That two-way connection makes it easy to jump from a concept to the tool that implements it — and back again.
</Tip>
