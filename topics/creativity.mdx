Creativity is worth studying not to demystify it, but to work with it more deliberately. This section of your wiki is where you capture what you've learned about the creative process — from techniques used by writers and designers to the mindsets and conditions that help original ideas emerge. Documenting your creative knowledge gives you something to return to when you're stuck, a record of what has sparked your best work, and a growing vocabulary for talking about creativity in any discipline you practice or admire.

## Creative Process Entry Format

Use this template when documenting a creative concept, technique, or lesson learned from a creator you admire:

```md
## [Creative Concept or Technique]
**Discipline:** Writing / Design / Music / etc.
**Source:** Book, artist, or personal discovery.
**Summary:** What the concept or technique is.
**How I Apply It:** Personal practice or experiment.
**Inspirations:** Works or creators that embody this.
```

<Tip>
  When a creative entry sparks a new project idea — a story concept, a design direction, an experiment you want to try — drop a note in your **Ideas** page right away. Keeping that connection live means your creativity notes don't just inform how you think, they actively feed what you make.
</Tip>
