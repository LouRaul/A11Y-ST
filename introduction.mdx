A personal knowledge wiki is a living document — a place where you collect, connect, and refine everything you learn over time. Unlike a simple folder of files or a pile of bookmarks, a well-organized wiki lets ideas build on each other, surfaces patterns you might otherwise miss, and gives you a reliable second brain to consult whenever you need it. This wiki is yours to explore, extend, and make your own.

## How It's Organized

This wiki follows a simple organizational philosophy built around three principles: **topics**, **tags**, and **references**. Each entry belongs to a top-level topic — a broad subject area that acts as its home. Within topics, tags let you draw connections across entries that share a theme even if they live in different sections. Finally, every entry is encouraged to link generously to related pages, building a web of references that rewards exploration. Rather than enforcing a rigid hierarchy, this structure stays flexible enough to grow naturally as your knowledge expands.
